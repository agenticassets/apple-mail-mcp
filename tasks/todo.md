**Native reply, live-measured 2026-08-24.** [`live-timing-and-frontmost-2026-08-24.md`](active/native-reply/live-timing-and-frontmost-2026-08-24.md) is the current note for this lane. Shipped: Mail is now polled to the front before the reply command and on every guard attempt (`REPLY_MAIL_NOT_FRONTMOST`), live-proven with another app deliberately holding the front. Measured over four successful live replies (1, 1, 21, 39 chunks): **0.70 s per chunk over 34.2 s of fixed overhead**, R2 0.98. The timeout projection models 1.0 s/chunk, so it over-projects by ~43% and the margin stays positive at every admissible length — a 3,060-char reply used 41% of its budget. `_NATIVE_TYPING_FIXED_OVERHEAD_SECONDS` was corrected 20 → 35 to match the measurement (the 30 s slack had been covering the gap), and `NativeReplyTimeoutCalibrationTests` now pins the constants against the live numbers instead of deriving expectations from the constants under test. Open, in priority order: (1) `REPLY_BODY_MISMATCH` is a normalizer ordering bug — `flattenForCompare` strips line breaks before folding paragraph starts, so an autocapitalized paragraph after a non-terminal-punctuation line fails a case-sensitive compare; (2) ~30.8 s of the 34.2 s fixed overhead is un-localized Mail/AX work and is the only real latency target.

**Native reply, LIVE-MEASURED 2026-08-25 — read this first.** [`live-results-2026-08-25.md`](active/native-reply/live-results-2026-08-25.md). Live testing ran to completion and found **three root causes, all fixed in the working tree and all confirmed by live runs**. (1) The Accessibility preflight took a single sample of Mail's window count and aborted on zero, blaming a sleeping display or a lapsed grant. Measured: Accessibility reports **0 windows for Mail whenever Mail sits on a different Space** — which any full-screen app creates — while Mail's own dictionary reports 8, with a ~0.3 s transition race where `frontmost` is already true. One run aborted at 0.79 s inside that gap. **This explains the reported "degrades over time" symptom with no accumulation at all, and the fix is to leave full-screen, not to restart Mail.** (2) Every truncation was a clean prefix with **no substitution**, always cutting mid-chunk: `keystroke` returns when events are posted, the loop skips its delay after the final chunk, so the script went straight to `save` and the undrained tail never reached the draft. Now settled via `waitForTypedBodyToSettle`. (3) **That wait had to scale with body length.** A flat 6 s budget only moved the boundary — 5,000 chars at chunk 300 lost 1,821 characters at 6 s and passed at 50 s, so **the tail was late, not dropped**. The budget now derives from `bodyLength`, and `_native_reply_effective_timeout` adds the same drain to its projection so `AppleScriptTimeout` cannot fire mid-drain. **`TYPING_CHUNK_SIZE` raised 120 → 300.** Chunk size was never a safety dial: it is a proxy for backlog depth, and once the drain is waited for, chunk 600 is *three times slower* than 300 (72.6 s vs 22.9 s) rather than more dangerous. 400 and 500 also passed cleanly but have one observation each. Closed since: the settle poll now exits on a **length delta** against a pre-typing baseline (the `contains bodyTail` tail match alone rarely fired, because WebKit rewrites characters in the editor, so the poll behaved as a scaled sleep). The tail exit survives only as a fallback and is gated off when the pre-typing editor already contains the tail, so a sign-off repeated in Mail's quoted original cannot satisfy it before a single character lands.

**Native reply, background on why the previous sweep was void — itself partly superseded, read the paragraph above first.** [`session-degradation-test-plan-2026-08-25.md`](active/native-reply/session-degradation-test-plan-2026-08-25.md) supersedes § 4 of the 2026-08-24 note. It was written *before* the drain fix and reverted `TYPING_CHUNK_SIZE` to **120**; the shipped value is now **300**. The sweep that shipped 160 was invalid: with ~1,126 drafts against a `DRAFT_LIST_CAP` of 75, the identity resolver bails and the verifier falls back to subject + body-contains, so runs typing identical bodies verified against *each other's* drafts. Every row carried `exact_id_verified: false`. **The "cliff between 160 and 200" is withdrawn**; the real boundary is unlocated and may sit between 120 and 160. What survives only as an observation, not as a mechanism: 200 and 250 did fail at 2,400 characters (contamination can only manufacture passes, never failures) — but nothing was substituted, the failures were clean prefixes of undrained text, and chunk 200 passes once the drain is waited for. Also withdrawn: a "14x Accessibility regression" that was load average 103.84 from concurrent subagents, and an `initializeAccessibility` "storm" that is a WebKit logging bug. Refuted outright: leaked `osascript` processes (zero at every sample) and an AppleSpell memory leak (`footprint` flat; `ps` RSS overstated it 6x). **Mail recovered without a restart.** The chunk-size question is settled for now at 300; do not run another *sweep* until the verifier fix (AGENTIC-2517) lands, because until then a repeated identical body can verify against a previous run's draft. Linear: **AGENTIC-2522** (tracking, carries the full state-of-knowledge table), AGENTIC-2517 (verifier accepts a foreign draft), AGENTIC-2518 (draft cap + false verification timeout), AGENTIC-2519 (compose-window cap blind on Tahoe), **AGENTIC-2520** (inter-chunk delay sits 50 ms above WebKit's 300 ms autocorrect timer — the likely real fix), AGENTIC-2521 (our own `entire contents` AX walk).
